## Chosen Approach — Terminal Field Notes

### Design Movement
**Swiss editorial systems interpreted as a developer’s field notebook.** The site borrows the assertive typography, measured asymmetry, and information hierarchy of technical print design, then adds restrained terminal behavior instead of decorative cyberpunk effects.

### Core Principles
1. **Signal over spectacle:** Neon green is used as a precise locator, not an ambient glow.
2. **Measured asymmetry:** Content follows a staggered editorial column rather than a centered marketing layout.
3. **Systems made visible:** Deliberate linework, status labels, and data-like microcopy quietly reveal structure.
4. **Work earns attention:** Projects and writing have substantial visual weight; ornament stays low-contrast and functional.

### Color Philosophy
The near-black base provides focus and makes the work feel like a focused coding environment. Slate-gray surfaces establish quiet hierarchy without turning the page into a grid of cards. **Signal Green** (#C7FF40) is a single, highly legible ownership color that directs attention to active states, key metrics, and primary calls to action.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is arranged as a **field report on a vertical rail**: a narrow left index column holds section labels and a fine vertical trace, while the primary content shifts in width and alignment across the right. Project cards interrupt the rhythm in a two-up composition, and the closing contact section expands into an inviting full-width panel.

### Signature Elements
1. A thin vertical **signal rail** with section codes and small green nodes.
2. Bracketed terminal labels such as `[SELECTED_WORK]` and `[AVAILABLE_FOR_COLLAB]`.
3. Hairline blueprint rules with short green tick marks at intentional intervals.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like navigating a well-built developer tool: direct, tactile, and informative. Links reveal their direction with a compact arrow shift; cards lift by only a few pixels and brighten their border. Navigation uses smooth anchoring, but all movement remains secondary to clarity.

### Animation
Use a restrained 180–260ms custom ease-out for hover and entrance states. On load, key content may fade upward by 8px in a short stagger, while the signal rail draws in once from top to bottom. Buttons compress to 97% on press. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by showing all content immediately and disabling nonessential transforms.

### Typography System
**Space Grotesk** provides the display voice: structural, open, and contemporary. **IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for labels, metadata, skill values, and code-like annotations. Headlines use tightly tracked, bold Space Grotesk; body copy remains spacious and readable; monospace text stays compact and letterspaced for a clear information layer.

### Brand Essence
**Marcus Chen builds dependable, thoughtfully engineered web products for teams that value clarity as much as capability.**

Personality: **methodical, curious, sharp.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, concrete, and slightly technical. Calls to action state the next meaningful action without generic optimism. Microcopy reads like a useful system status.

Example lines:

> “Full-stack systems, built to hold up.”

> “Read the implementation notes →”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a compact **MC monogram reduced to two interlocking terminal brackets**, forming a square signal glyph. It appears at a generous scale in the navigation and as a simplified favicon; the plain wordmark is paired with it in Space Grotesk rather than treated as the logo itself.
